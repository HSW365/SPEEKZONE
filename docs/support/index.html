Common questions

How do I create an account?

Open SpeekZone, tap Sign Up, and enter a name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters. Your account is created instantly and you're signed in.

I can't sign in or sign up.

Make sure you have an active internet connection and your password is at least 8 characters. If it still fails, email us the exact message you see and we'll resolve it quickly.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → menu icon (top right) → Delete Account. This is immediate and permanent. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

How do I cancel my Verified subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Cancel anytime: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SpeekZone → Cancel.

How do I report a room, clip, or user?

Use the report option in the app, or email us with details and we'll investigate promptly.

What does Verified include?

SpeekZone is free to use. Verified ($9.99/month) adds a verification checkmark, creator analytics, priority placement in Discover, a custom profile banner, and gift revenue-share eligibility. It doesn't gate any core feature.
